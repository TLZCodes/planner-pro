## Canonical descriptor

A portrait phone frame used as the presentation device for every captured screen in the film. The body is a solid, warm dark plum mast — not black, not grey — reading as approximately `#40263b` under the film's soft high-key light, with no gloss, no reflection and no visible material texture. The frame is a single rounded rectangle whose corner radius is 44 device-independent pixels at a 300 × 614 pixel body size, giving a radius-to-width ratio of roughly 0.147. The body is drawn as a border of uniform thickness: 9 device-independent pixels of plum on all four sides, with the inner screen region inset by that same 9 pixels and carrying a 36 device-independent pixel corner radius, so the screen's corners are visibly tighter than the body's. There is no chin and no forehead: the border thickness is identical on every edge. A cut-away pill sits centred horizontally with its top edge 9 device-independent pixels below the top of the screen region; the pill is 96 pixels wide and 20 pixels high with a fully rounded 10 pixel radius, and it is drawn in the same plum as the body so it reads as a cut into the device rather than an added element. The screen region itself is never drawn by this asset: it is a clip window that holds a real captured application screen, and the frame must therefore never render fake interface content, fake icons, fake status bars or fake text of its own. The only shadow is a single soft ambient drop beneath and slightly behind the body — two layers, the larger being 26 pixels of vertical offset with a 60 pixel blur at 24% opacity in a plum-tinted shadow colour, the smaller being 6 pixels of offset with a 14 pixel blur at 14% opacity — so the device appears to float a few millimetres above the blush ground rather than rest on a surface. There is no highlight along the top edge, no side button, no camera lens other than the pill cut-away, no brand imprint, no port, and no visible seam. The frame is always presented perfectly vertical: zero rotation, zero perspective, no tilt, and no reflective environment map. It is not held by a hand and no human presence is implied at any point in the film.
