## Canonical descriptor

More hub and Settings. Layout: The More hub is a directory: module cards grouped under category headers, where each card carries an emoji or icon, the module's title, a one-line description and a dynamic stat line that reflects the user's own records. Cards are laid out two per row in a responsive grid, and pinned modules may be hoisted to a featured group at the top. The Settings section opens as grouped rows, each group holding labelled rows with trailing controls: a theme selector offering the app's colour themes, a language selector offering English and Arabic with its text direction, a region group holding time zone and currency, a reminders group holding notification behaviour, and a data group holding export and restore as file operations. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the category headers, the module cards with their stat lines, the pinned group, and the Settings group rows with their trailing controls. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the category headers as the directory's structure, the module cards as its entries, and the language and theme rows as the tie between the app and the film's own closing argument. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Module cards are roughly 178 by 158 pixels in a two-column grid with 12 pixels of gap; Settings rows sit in grouped cards with 2 pixel separators. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are the app's own directory styling: grouped flat paper cards, one accent per category header, and no additional graphic layer added by the film. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
