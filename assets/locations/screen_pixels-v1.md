## Canonical descriptor

Year in Pixels. Layout: A tab row separates year, month and insights. The year view is a dense grid of one cell per day of the year, arranged in columns of days and rows of months, each cell a small rounded square filled with the mood colour recorded for that day and left as an empty outlined cell when no mood was recorded. A mood legend sits beneath or beside the grid, listing each mood name against its swatch, and a summary row may show the distribution across moods. A settings affordance opens palette and cell-shape options and an export action. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the day grid, the empty cells that represent unrecorded days, the mood legend and the mood distribution summary. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the grid's left edge as the reading anchor, the month boundaries as the grid's structure, and the legend as the colour key that makes the mosaic readable. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Grid cells are 9 to 12 pixel rounded squares with 2 to 3 pixel gaps so a full year fits the width; the legend swatches match the cell size. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are a flat mosaic of pastel mood colours held directly on the cream ground with no card behind the grid, so the pattern is the only object. No texture, no gradient, no border around the whole mosaic. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
