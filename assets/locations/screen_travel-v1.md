## Canonical descriptor

Travel planner. Layout: A tab row separates trips from a bucket list. The trips view opens with a trip header card carrying the trip title, the origin and primary city, the date range, the traveller count and a status tag, with a compact budget summary beneath. An itinerary section follows, grouped by day: each day is a sub-header with its local date and title, and beneath it the day's items are rows carrying a start time, an item title, an optional location name and a type indicator. A packing checklist section follows, where categories hold checkable rows with a title and a quantity, and a booking list closes the screen with each booking showing its provider, its dates, its estimated amount and a payment status tag. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the trip header card, the per-day itinerary sub-headers and item rows, the packing checklist categories, and the booking rows with their payment status tags. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the trip header at the top, the day sub-headers as the itinerary's spine, and the booking payment tags as the tie to the finance ledger. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. The trip header is the tallest card on the screen at roughly 190 pixels; itinerary item rows sit in grouped cards with 2 pixel separators. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper cards with a powder-blue accent on the trip header and a small hand-drawn route motif placed behind the header text, never over it. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
