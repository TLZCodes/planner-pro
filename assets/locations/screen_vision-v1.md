## Canonical descriptor

Vision Board. Layout: A pastel canvas holding freely arranged scrapbook cards rather than a strict grid. Each card is a paper tile carrying one of four contents: a photograph, a short text or affirmation, a wish, or a card explicitly linked to a goal, in which case it also shows that goal's title and a compact progress indicator. Cards are individually rotated by between minus three and plus three degrees, carry a caption strip beneath the content, and are decorated with one of a small set of motifs, being a flower, a star, a bow, a sparkle or a heart, plus an occasional torn washi strip in pink, lavender, butter, mint or a striped pattern. The board's own header holds its title, its time horizon and a small settings affordance; a board switcher may appear when more than one board exists. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the board canvas, the freely placed cards, the per-card caption strips and motif decorations, the washi strips, the board header, and an add-item action pinned above the bottom navigation. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the board header at the top, the cards' individual rotations as the canvas's visual grain, and the linked-goal cards as the board's tie back to structured planning. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Cards vary in size by content, from roughly 150 by 150 pixels for a square photograph to roughly 300 by 130 pixels for a text card, with 12 pixels of typical overlap-free spacing. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are paper tiles with torn or straight edges, subtle rotation, and washi and motif decoration placed behind and beside the content rather than over it. Weathering is deliberate and limited: torn edges and slight rotation, never grunge, never stains, never anything that reduces text contrast. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
