## Canonical descriptor

Habits. Layout: A view switcher opens the screen, offering a daily list and a weekly board. In the weekly board, a seven-column grid is the dominant object: the leading column is the habit column, holding each habit's emoji and name in a stacked row, and the remaining seven columns are the week's days, each cell being a small rounded square that is filled when the habit is due and completed, outlined when due and not completed, and left empty when the habit is not scheduled for that day. A per-habit row may additionally carry a small progress figure in the trailing cell of the habit column. Above the grid sits a week-range header with navigation to the previous and next week, and below the grid sit the preference cards that control first day of week, grouping, density and which side panels are shown. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the view switcher, the week-range header, the grid's leading habit column, the seven day columns, and the preference cards. A habit creation action sits above the bottom navigation. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the week-range header as the top boundary, the habit column as the grid's left edge and reading anchor, and the weekly progress figure as the grid's summary. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Grid cells are 26 to 30 pixel rounded squares with 4 pixel gaps; the habit column is roughly 118 pixels wide so a two-word name remains readable. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are a paper card holding the grid, with each cell drawn flat. The paper theme is selectable and changes the card fill rather than the material. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
