Content on the page is drawn freehand or typed and includes headings, lists, boxed sections and hand-drawn rules, together with placed stickers and a torn washi strip where the author has used one. A tool bar sits above or below the page holding pen, highlighter, eraser and selection tools, along with a small colour set drawn from the film's pastel palette; a sticker strip sits adjacent to the tool bar, showing the available motifs as small tiles. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the page canvas with its rule, the tool bar, the colour set and the sticker strip. The page scrolls vertically; the tool bar is pinned. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the page's own rule as its texture, the tool bar as the working edge, and the sticker strip as the decorative source. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. The dot grid runs at roughly 22 pixel spacing; sticker tiles are roughly 44 pixels square. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are pastel paper with dotted rules, a torn washi strip and small flower motifs, all placed behind the writing so nothing reduces the legibility of what is written. Weathering is intentional and bounded: a torn edge and a hand-drawn line, never grunge, never a stained or aged page. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
