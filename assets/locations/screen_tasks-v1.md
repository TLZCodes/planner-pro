## Canonical descriptor

Tasks. Layout: A vertical scroll column of task groups separated by small uppercase group headers. The first group is the top-three focus block, containing exactly three cards, each with a leading prioritised checkbox, a title, an optional time chip and the category tag. The second group is the time-scheduled list, ordered ascending by optional time, with the time chip visible on every card. The third group is the anytime list, whose cards show no time chip at all. A completed group may follow, and completed cards are rendered with a reduced-opacity title and a struck-through line. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the group headers, the top-three block, the time-scheduled list, the anytime list and the completion affordance on every card. A floating primary action sits above the bottom navigation on the trailing edge for creating a task. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the top-three header as the topmost boundary of the list, the transition from time-scheduled to anytime cards as the middle divider, and the floating action as the trailing-edge anchor. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Cards are full width within an 18 pixel gutter, 24 pixel radius, with 10 pixels of vertical gap inside a group and 22 pixels between groups. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat white paper cards with hairline borders; no visible grain, no inner shadow, no texture. Completed cards are the same card with a slightly muted fill rather than a different material. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
