## Canonical descriptor

My Deen. Layout: A today view opens the screen: a next-prayer card shows the upcoming prayer's name, its time and a countdown, followed by a horizontal row of the day's five prayer times, each in its own compact tile with the prayer name above the time. Below that sits a Quran-wird progress card showing pages read against a daily target, then an Athkar card offering morning and evening sets, then a Tasbih counter card with a large numeral and a phrase, then a Sadaqah card, then a good-deeds list whose rows are checkable for the current day. A horizontally scrollable tab row above the today view separates today, Quran, Athkar, Prayers, Tasbih, Sadaqah and good deeds. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the section tab row, the next-prayer card, the five prayer-time tiles, the Quran progress card, the Athkar card, the Tasbih counter, the Sadaqah card and the good-deeds rows. A settings affordance for calculation method, madhab, location and Hijri adjustment sits in the header. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the next-prayer card as the screen's first read, the row of five prayer tiles as the day's spine, and the section tab row as the navigation above all of it. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Prayer-time tiles are roughly 66 by 74 pixels; the Tasbih numeral is rendered at display size inside a card of roughly 210 pixels height. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are warm neutral paper cards with a soft green accent used sparingly on the Qur'anic and prayer elements. Flat, untextured, and deliberately quieter than the rest of the film: no celebratory treatment, no metallic ornament, no photographic imagery of people or places. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream ground `#fff8fa` with white paper cards `#ffffff`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text `#2b1727`; the accent colour is a soft desaturated green used only on the prayer and Qur'an elements. Green is held below clipping so it reads as calm rather than as a signal. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
