## Canonical descriptor

Learning hub. Layout: A tab row opens the screen across subjects, books, flashcards and plan. Beneath it, the subject list runs as a column of cards, each carrying an emoji, a title, a thin progress rule showing study time against a weekly target, and the tracked minutes as numerals. A study heatmap sits below the subject list: a grid of one cell per day over the recent period, each cell shaded according to logged study minutes, with weekday labels along the leading edge and a small legend beneath. A book library section follows, where each book is a card carrying its cover emoji, title, author and a page-progress rule. Flashcard decks appear as cards carrying a deck name, a due count and a new count, with a review affordance. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the subject cards, the study heatmap, the legend, the book cards and the flashcard deck cards. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the tab row at the top, the progress rule inside each subject card as its midline, and the heatmap as the screen's proof of real logged time. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Heatmap cells are 14 pixel rounded squares with 4 pixel gaps; subject cards are full width at 24 pixel radius. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper cards with a single soft blue accent reserved for the heatmap's shading ramp. No texture, no gradient, no photograph. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
