## Canonical descriptor

Ideal Week and Between the Prayers. Layout: A header block opens the screen with the title and a short subtitle. Directly beneath it sits a view switcher holding two adjacent actions, one for the reusable template and one for the dated week plan. Below the switcher a planning-mode selector holds three adjacent actions: time of day, clock time, and between the prayers, with the active mode filled and the inactive modes on the surface fill. Beneath the mode selector sits a seven-cell day strip, each cell carrying a weekday abbreviation, the day's date and a small day-type pill, with the selected cell visually raised. The remainder of the screen holds the selected day's plan for the active mode: in time of day mode, period groups with items inside them; in clock mode, rows with a start time and an optional duration; in between-the-prayers mode, six period cards listed in order, each carrying a prayer-relative label, the two resolved prayer times for the selected date and any items placed inside it. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the view switcher, the mode selector, the seven-cell day strip and the selected day's plan region. A reset-to-starter affordance sits in the header on the trailing edge, and an apply action appears only in the dated-plan view. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the view switcher as the boundary between template and dated plan, the mode selector as the boundary between the three planning grammars, the day strip as the week's spine, and the six period cards as the prayer-relative sequence. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Day-strip cells are roughly 52 by 62 pixels; period cards are full width at 22 pixel radius with 10 pixels of gap. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are pastel paper cards on the blush ground, flat and untextured, with the decorative accent held to small motifs at the card corners. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
