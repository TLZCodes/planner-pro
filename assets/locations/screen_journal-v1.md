## Canonical descriptor

Journal. Layout: A tab row separates free entries from templates and from the weekly reset. In the entry list, each entry is a paper card carrying the date on its leading edge, the entry's title, a first-line excerpt of the body, a mood emoji, a small energy indicator and any tags as compact chips. A locked entry is the same card with its body replaced by a quiet sealed state and a small lock indicator, and it carries no excerpt, no mood emoji and no tags. A search or filter affordance sits near the header, and a compose action floats above the bottom navigation. The weekly reset tab, when open, replaces the list with a four-step studio whose current step is shown by a step indicator, and whose body region holds the active step's content. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the entry cards, the locked-entry sealed state, the compose action and, on the weekly reset tab, the step indicator. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the tab row as the boundary between journaling and reviewing, the locked card as the privacy marker, and the step indicator as the weekly reset's position. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Entry cards are full width at 24 pixel radius with 12 pixels of gap; the step indicator is a row of four equal cells. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper cards, with the locked card rendered on a slightly muted fill so its sealed state is legible without any padlock imagery beyond a small indicator. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
