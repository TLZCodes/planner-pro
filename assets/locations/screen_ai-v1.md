## Canonical descriptor

AI planner conversation. Layout: A conversation column with message bubbles alternating between user and assistant, each bubble being a paper card with a 24 pixel radius and a maximum width of roughly 78% of the viewport, aligned to the trailing edge for the user and the leading edge for the assistant. Below the message list, and fixed to the bottom edge of the scroll area, sits a composer card containing a multiline text field with placeholder text and a circular send action. Structured proposals sent by the assistant appear inside assistant bubbles as an embedded list of rows, each row showing a proposed title, an optional proposed time and a proposed category tag, with the acceptance affordance rendered on the proposal itself. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the message list, the composer card, the circular send action and the embedded proposal rows. The composer is pinned rather than scrolled, and the proposal rows are the only interactive elements inside a bubble. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the leading-edge versus trailing-edge bubble alignment as the conversation's axis, and the composer as the pinned baseline. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Bubbles carry 24 pixel radius with 10 pixels of gap between turns; the composer is a single full-width card with a 28 pixel radius. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper bubbles with hairline borders and no gradient, no glow and no artificial shimmer on the assistant side. The assistant bubble is not visually magical: no sparkle overlays, no animated typing indicator, no neon border. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
