## Canonical descriptor

Focus. Layout: A session dial dominates the upper half of the screen: a large ring whose track is drawn at low opacity and whose progress arc is drawn in the accent, with the remaining or elapsed time rendered large inside the ring as numerals, accompanied by a smaller line naming the current phase. Beneath the dial sits a row of mode and preset controls, then a source selector that binds the session to a task, a subject, a goal, a business project or nothing at all, then a card holding the ambient sound selector, whose rows each carry a sound name and a playing control. Session controls sit at the foot of the screen, with a primary start action taking the accent fill. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the dial, the phase label, the mode controls, the source selector, the ambient sound card and the session controls at the foot. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the dial as the screen's centre of gravity, the source selector as the line that ties the session to the rest of the app, and the sound card as the lower anchor. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. The dial is roughly 260 pixels in diameter with a ring stroke of 14 pixels; preset controls are pill shaped at 14 pixel radius. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are a calm paper card behind the dial and a flat card behind the sound rows, with a single warm accent used on the progress arc and the primary action. No glow, no pulsation, no texture. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
