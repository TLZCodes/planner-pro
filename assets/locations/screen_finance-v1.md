## Canonical descriptor

Finance. Layout: A tab row separates an overview from transactions, budgets, savings and settings. The overview opens with a net position card showing total assets, total liabilities and the net figure, each on its own line. Beneath it sits an account list, each account a row carrying its icon, name, type and current balance on the trailing edge. A budget card follows, carrying the month, an overall progress rule against the overall limit, and per-category rules for the categories that have limits. A savings section lists savings goals with their target, contributed amount and a progress rule. A privacy affordance for masking displayed amounts sits in the header. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the net position card, the account rows, the budget card with its category rules, the savings goal rows and the masking affordance. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the net position card as the screen's first read, the account rows as the balance spine, and the budget card as the month's boundary. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. The net position card is full width at 26 pixel radius; balance figures are rendered as the largest numerals on the screen. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are ledger-clean flat paper cards with a mint accent used on positive figures and on the budget rules. Amounts are rendered in the same type as every other figure so no amount shouts. No currency symbols are oversized and no figure is decorated. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
