## Canonical descriptor

Routines and Ideal Day. Layout: A horizontally scrollable tab row runs directly beneath the top of the screen, holding one pill per routine with a short label and a small emoji. Beneath the tab row sits a hero banner card for the selected routine: a large card carrying the routine's title, a completion percentage rendered large, a thin progress rule, and a small secondary line describing the routine's span. Below the hero, the step list runs as a single column of rows, where each row contains a time or prayer anchor on the leading edge, an emoji, the step title, and a trailing completion control. Section headers divide the steps into morning, day, afternoon, evening and night groupings when the routine spans the whole day. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the routine tab row, the hero banner, the per-section headers and the step rows. The guided-mode affordance is attached to the hero banner rather than floating, and a routine creation action sits at the trailing end of the tab row. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the tab row as the screen's top boundary, the hero banner as the mid-frame anchor, and the first section header as the beginning of the step sequence. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. The hero banner is a full-width card at 28 pixel radius; step rows sit in a grouped card with 24 pixel radius and 2 pixel separators between rows. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are pastel paper cards, flat and untextured, with decorative linework deliberately placed behind the card layer and never over content. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
