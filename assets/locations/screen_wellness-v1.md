## Canonical descriptor

Wellness hub. Layout: A tab row separates today from trackers, movement and insights. The today view opens with a hydration panel showing a container-progress visual with consumed millilitres against the target, followed by a quick-add row of glass and bottle amounts. A sleep panel follows, showing the most recent night's duration, its quality rating and its wake date. A movement panel follows with the day's step count and any logged workouts as rows carrying the activity type, duration and intensity. A mood panel closes the view, showing the day's mood emoji, its energy level and a compact entry control. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the hydration panel with its container visual, the quick-add row, the sleep panel, the movement panel and the mood panel. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the hydration panel as the screen's first read, the sleep panel as the middle anchor, and the mood panel as the foot. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Panels are full-width cards at 26 pixel radius; the hydration container visual is roughly 56 pixels tall. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are soft mint and powder-blue panels on the cream ground, each panel drawing from one shared border and one shared radius so the four read as a family rather than four separate tools. No texture, no gradient beyond the container fill. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
