## Canonical descriptor

Challenges. Layout: A list of available protocols opens the screen, offering seven-day, thirty-day, seventy-five-day and ninety-day lengths as cards, each carrying the protocol's title, its length, a one-line description and a selection action. Beneath the catalogue, a running protocol card shows the active challenge's title, its start date, its total length and its day tracker: a grid of one cell per day, where completed days are filled, the current day is marked distinctly, and future days are drawn as empty outlined cells. A set of that day's tasks sits beneath the tracker, each a checkable row, and a progress figure states the completed days against the total. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the protocol catalogue cards, the running protocol card, the day tracker grid, the day's task rows and the progress figure. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the catalogue at the top, the running protocol card as the screen's centre, and the day tracker with its current-day marker as the honesty anchor. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Tracker cells are 22 to 26 pixel rounded squares with 4 pixel gaps; protocol cards are full width at 24 pixel radius. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper cards with a bold accent used only on the protocol title and the current-day marker, and a deliberately neutral grid for the tracker so a missed day is neither hidden nor punished visually. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
