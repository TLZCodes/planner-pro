## Canonical descriptor

Home dashboard. Layout: A single vertical scroll column with 18 pixels of side gutter at a 412 pixel viewport width. The column runs, top to bottom: a time-aware greeting line with the user's name on a second line; a dhikr or reminder card immediately beneath it; a horizontally scrolling quick-action rail of squircle icon buttons; the today's-plan card group, where each task is one full-width paper card with a leading completion circle, a title line and a right-aligned time chip with an optional category tag beneath it; a statistics grid of four compact tiles in two rows of two showing completed tasks, habit streak, focus minutes and study minutes; and a routines progress card group, where each routine is one card with its name, a thin progress rule and a completion percentage. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the greeting block, the dhikr card, the quick-action rail, the task card group, the two-by-two statistics grid and the routine progress cards. The quick-action rail is the only horizontally scrollable element on the screen, and it clips its own overflow rather than the page's. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the greeting at the top of the scroll, the dhikr card as the second element, and the statistics grid as the boundary between the day's plan and the routine progress. There is no persistent header bar and no back control: this is a root destination, reachable only from the bottom navigation. Scale: Scale is a 412 by 915 device-independent pixel viewport photographed at twice density. Paper cards are 24 to 32 pixel radius with a one-pixel hairline border and a very low elevation shadow, and they sit in a single column with 12 pixels of vertical gap between siblings. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat, untextured and unmoving: paper cards with no visible grain at capture resolution, a hairline border at roughly 6% plum, and no inner shadow. Weathering is absent by design; nothing on this screen is scuffed, torn, folded or stained, because the film's screens are the product as it actually renders. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
