## Canonical descriptor

Business workspace. Layout: A tab row separates an overview from projects, clients, offers and invoices. The overview opens with a summary band of compact tiles showing project count, active client count, open invoice value and paid value. Beneath the band sits a project list, each project a card carrying its name, a progress rule, a progress percentage, a target date and its next milestone. A client section follows, each client a row with a name, a company, a status tag and a trailing follow-up date, and an invoice section lists invoices with their number, client, total, paid amount and a status pill. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the tab row, the summary tiles, the project cards with their milestone lines, the client rows and the invoice rows with their status pills. Creation actions for projects, clients, offers and invoices sit above the bottom navigation. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the tab row at the top, the summary band as the screen's first read, and the invoice status pills as the tie between business work and money. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Summary tiles are laid out two per row; project and client cards are full width at 22 to 24 pixel radius. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are ledger-clean flat paper cards with a restrained gold accent used only on monetary figures and status pills. No celebratory treatment, no confetti, no glow. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
