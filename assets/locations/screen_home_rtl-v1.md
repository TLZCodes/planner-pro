## Canonical descriptor

Home dashboard, mirrored. Layout: Structurally identical to `@screen_home` and mirrored about the vertical axis. The scroll column's gutter is symmetric so the mirror is not merely a text alignment change: the greeting and its second line are right-aligned with their leading edge at the right gutter, the dhikr card's internal content is right-aligned, the quick-action rail scrolls right to left with its first item at the right edge, each task card has its completion circle at the right edge and its time chip at the left, the statistics grid preserves its two-by-two arrangement with each tile's numerals right-aligned, and the routine progress cards place their percentage at the left. The bottom navigation is mirrored: Home sits at the right end of the bar and More at the left, with the raised centre action still centred. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the mirrored greeting block, the dhikr card, the quick-action rail, the task card group, the statistics grid, the routine progress cards, and the mirrored five-slot bottom navigation. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the greeting at the top right as the reading entry point, the mirrored task cards as the clearest evidence of true mirroring rather than translation, and the reversed bottom navigation as the persistent shell's own mirror. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Card sizes, radii and gaps are numerically identical to `@screen_home`; only the horizontal order and the text alignment differ. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are the identical visual system to the English screens: same cream ground, same white paper, same hairline borders, same radii. The only change is direction. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
