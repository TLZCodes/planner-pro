## Canonical descriptor

Goals. Layout: A vertical list of goal cards, each card a paper surface carrying an emoji, a title, an area tag, a thin progress bar, a numeric progress figure on the trailing edge, a target label, and a next-action line rendered as a distinct sub-row with a small arrow cue. An expanded goal card additionally reveals a milestone list, where each milestone is a checkable row with a title and an optional due date, and a check-in affordance that opens a dated progress entry. A tab row may sit above the list separating active, focus and completed goals. Fixed architecture: Fixed objects are the goal cards, the per-card progress rule, the next-action sub-row, the expanded milestone list and the check-in affordance. A goal creation action sits above the bottom navigation. Spatial landmarks: Spatial landmarks are the goal title at the head of each card, the progress rule as the card's midline, and the next-action row as the card's foot. Scale: Scale is the same 412 by 915 viewport at twice density. Goal cards are full width within an 18 pixel gutter at 26 pixel radius; the progress rule is 6 pixels tall. Surfaces and weathering: Surfaces are flat paper cards, with the milestone rows inset as a slightly recessed sub-surface inside the parent card. No texture, no gradient. Weathering is absent. Dominant colour is the cream blush ground `#fff8fa` carrying white paper cards `#ffffff` on a slightly pinker surface `#ffeef4`; the secondary colour is the dark plum text and hairline borders read at `#2b1727`, appearing as the ink on every card; the accent colour is the surface's own, used only on the active control, the progress figure or the primary action. Light character is soft, even and high-key with no visible key direction: the screen is self-lit and perfectly flat, with no cast shadow inside the interface, no vignette, no glare, and no reflection. The built-in grade is a gentle warm-neutral lift with the blacks held just above pure black so plum text stays soft rather than crushing, greens kept slightly desaturated, and skin-adjacent pinks held below clipping so blush never blooms.
